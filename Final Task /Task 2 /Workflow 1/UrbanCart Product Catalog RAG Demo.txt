3. DELIVERY INFORMATION

UrbanCart delivers within Pakistan. Standard delivery times depend on location and the applicable shipping policy. The AI should consult the current shipping-policy document for delivery-time questions rather than relying on assumptions from this catalog.

For a question about whether a particular location is served, the AI should use the current shipping policy. If the required location information is not available, it should not guess.

4. PRODUCT AVAILABILITY RULES

- Products marked In Stock can currently be presented as available.
- Products marked Limited Stock should be described as having limited availability.
- Products marked Out of Stock should not be presented as currently available.
- Availability can change and the AI should use the latest available business data.
- If the requested product is not present in the catalog, the AI must not invent a product, price or availability status.
